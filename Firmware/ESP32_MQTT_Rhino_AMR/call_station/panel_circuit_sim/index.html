Panel circuit simulator — ESP32-S3 Call Station

Clean schematic: wires meet pins on box edges (no crossing through boxes). White = power latch. Blue = latching Task-14 switch (press → CLOSED, press again → OPEN). GPIO is 3.3V only — never 5V on the blue switch.

Live state

Power Blue latch Green Yellow Red

Phase: idle — latch White ON, then latch Blue CLOSED for Task-14.

Blue = latching switch

Press 1 → contacts CLOSED (GPIO7 LOW) → Task-14 starts.
Press 2 → contacts OPEN (GPIO7 HIGH) → sequence stops.
Stays latched until you press again (not momentary).

Task-14 (while Blue CLOSED)

  1. Green ON — AMR starts from home
  2. Yellow ON — go to p2 pick pallet
  3. Red ON — returning home
  4. All OFF — done (Blue can stay CLOSED until you open it)

Mentor: 10k / 15k?

Blue: no series resistor (GPIO↔GND).
Do not wire 5V→switch→GPIO.
White: no 10k on battery path.
